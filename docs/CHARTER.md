# HAUNTED BLENDER — AAA MOONSHOT CHARTER

Status: charter v0.1, aspirational product definition. A named destination, **not** a claim that the implementation exists.

## North star

A filmmaker with a modest computer, an idea, and ten thousand already-edited photographs can create, revise, and distribute coherent cinematic stories. The same project can mature from a photographic storyboard to filmed drama, episodic series, virtual production, and collaborative studio work without throwing away the film's original scene history.

Haunted Blender is a separate sibling to Haunted Toaster: **photography/story/scene first**, not song/score first. It may use Toaster-derived and AgentBroko adapters by explicit contract, and use Blender 3D for virtual production later; no engine owns story identity or silently mutates accepted intent.

## AAA means capability depth, not a promise about launch date or visual quality

- Deep creative control: film → act → scene → beat → shot → take → edit → export. Each layer can be independently revised and recomposed.
- Asset-scale production: 10k+ original photos and edited variants as the minimum real-world archive target, with incremental ingest, resilient resumability, search, identity-bound source provenance, and selective previews.
- Local-first, user-owned original files and projects. A fully offline workflow remains meaningful. Cloud generation is optional, per-shot, priced/consented, with explicit disclosure of what data leaves the machine.
- Pluggable image, video, audio, 3D, motion and compositing engines that advertise actual capabilities; quality tiers never fabricate a capability.
- Human-directed dramaturgy: character, action, speech, dramatic question, continuity, intentional ambiguity and scene revision. A story suggestion cannot assert a private subject's identity, consent, motive or life history as fact.
- Incremental regeneration: re-rendering scene 4 does not invalidate scenes 1–3; locked shots stay locked unless explicitly unlocked.
- Accessible entry: a story idea can yield a small but coherent edited artifact without requiring users to understand nodes or a conventional NLE. Expert mode exposes all underlying control.
- Collaboration and repeatable production: source use rights, role-separated access, review/approval, manifests, stable exports, provenance and non-claims.

## Primary users

1. Independent drama filmmaker: script → scene breakdown → cast/locations → shots → performances → picture and sound editing → episodic production.
2. Personal photographic storyteller: years of edited photographs → local archive → discovery and curation → narrated film → more elaborate scene realization.
3. Documentary creator: factual source records, quotes, media, dates and permissions remain distinct from reconstructions and dramatic representations.
4. Music-video creator: intentional interoperation with Haunted Toaster without relocating Toaster's canonical score authority.

## Product laws

1. Source and derivative are different entities. CR2+XMP is not a Lightroom-rendered JPEG. Originals are immutable in application behavior, never committed to public source control, and never uploaded without explicit user opt-in.
2. A scene is a first-class durable creative object; a render is only one realization of a scene.
3. Preserve semantic intent and exact execution inputs using versioned snapshots; scene/shot IDs do not depend on rendering engine.
4. An AI suggestion is not an observation. Keep human-attested facts, model-inferred relationships, speculation, dramatic invention and unresolved matters distinguishable.
5. All external rendering models are subordinate to explicit capabilities and a declared local/remote boundary. No hidden credit charges.
6. Approval is scoped: a person can approve a shot, dialogue, asset use or export separately, and can revoke approval of future use without rewriting past provenance.
7. Failed/partial work cannot report successful completion. Receipts describe what demonstrably happened and explicit non-claims.
8. Human filmmaker retains narrative agency; no automated public release or irreversible overwrite.

## The five progressive workspaces

- **Spark** — brief, images, narration and a coherent short film with transparent simplifications.
- **Scene** — screenplay, beats, shot director, character references, editorial review, scene revision.
- **Studio** — reusable characters/locations/sets, continuity, editorial timeline, takes and episodic assets.
- **Stage** — structured environments, 2.5D/3D, lights/cameras, performance/motion, multi-engine rendering.
- **Production** — team permissions, pipelines, multi-episode catalog, collaborative approvals, delivery and quality control.

These are progressively revealed interfaces over a stable scene/shot/asset core, **not five unrelated codebases**.

## Product naming and boundary

- Product: **Haunted Blender**.
- Sibling: **Haunted Toaster** (music-first; current score/timeline/render contracts remain untouched).
- Optional 3D technology: Blender (the separate open-source 3D application; no claim of affiliation).
- Optional local procedural rendering technology: AgentBroko Video Forge (FFmpeg-based); the engine must be verified against the installed runtime before native integration.
- Sister assets may be shared through a deliberately versioned exchange layer; no hidden direct writes into sibling projects.
