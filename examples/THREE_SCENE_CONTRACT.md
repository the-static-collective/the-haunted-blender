# Future pilot: editable three-scene drama

A filmmaker selects privately owned source photographs and describes a fictional conflict. The application proposes a treatment; the filmmaker approves or revises actual source associations and dramatic roles.

Arrival — what is unsaid? → Kitchen — what changes? → Departure — what remains unresolved?

Each scene has an independent stable ID and editorial intent. One source photo may recur in multiple scenes; its creative use does not assert an identity or real-world event. A scene is the same narrative object as it develops from a silent still storyboard to performed and animated footage.

v0 only supports manually created scenes and silent static stills. Dialogue, full story authoring, moving performances and continuity checks are future work, not present capabilities.
