# Future pilot: three-scene drama

A filmmaker describes a conflict, names the fictional characters, and selects source photographs. Haunted Blender proposes a three-scene treatment where each scene has a dramatic question, an editable shot sequence and optional audio direction. A source photograph may recur in different scenes without losing its identity; a scene's narrative function remains separate from visual realization.

Sample scene chain: Arrival (what is unsaid?) → Kitchen (what changes?) → Departure (what remains unresolved?). These are fictional scaffolding questions, not assertions about real people appearing in any photographs.

At the v0 scaffold level, only manually created scenes and silent photo stills are rendered. Voice performance, animated motion, full story authoring, and continuity are explicit development targets, **not** current features.
